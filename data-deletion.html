Last updated: October 15, 2026

This page is for the autotest21 Instagram DM workflow test app.

Request deletion

If you sent a direct message to the connected test Instagram account and want to request deletion of related test data, contact the developer using the contact information associated with this app in Meta for Developers. Include that your request concerns the “autotest21 Instagram DM workflow test”. Do not include your password or access tokens.

The developer will review the request and delete test data under their control that is no longer needed, where practical. Some information may remain temporarily in service backups or logs, subject to the relevant service's retention controls and legal obligations.

To stop further processing through this test, do not send further messages to the test account.

This is a test app. Before real customer use, replace this page with the operator's actual contact details and deletion process.
