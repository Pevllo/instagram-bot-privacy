Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This page describes the handling of information for the autotest21 Instagram DM workflow test app, operated by the app developer for testing an automated Instagram messaging workflow.

Information processed

When a person sends a direct message to the connected Instagram test account, the app may process the message text and the sender/account identifier provided by Instagram. These details are used only to test message routing and automated replies.

How information is used

Message content may be checked against test keywords (for example, price, sizes, delivery, or returns) to choose a response. Messages that do not match a test category may receive a fallback reply. The workflow may process event data through the automation service configured by the developer.

Storage and sharing

This is a testing app. The developer does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be processed by Meta/Instagram and the automation or hosting services needed to operate the test. Workflow execution logs or service diagnostics may retain event data according to those services' settings. Do not send sensitive personal information to the test account.

Retention and security

Access is limited to the developer and authorized test users. The developer will delete test data when it is no longer needed, where practical, and will use available service controls to limit access. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

To stop participating, do not send further messages to the test account. To ask a question about information processed during this test, or to request deletion of related test data, contact the developer using the contact information associated with this app in Meta for Developers.

Children

This test is not intended for children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly seek to collect their information.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the test workflow changes. The date at the top indicates the most recent revision.

This policy is for the limited autotest21 workflow test. Before using the app for a business or real customers, update this policy to accurately describe the business, data retention, contact details, and applicable legal requirements.
